Chicken Recipe

Description

The chicken is the world’s most common domesticated fowl, primarily raised for its meat and eggs. Known for its versatility, mild flavor, and lean protein, it is celebrated globally as the "king of the kitchen" and is easily adapted to local staples like Chicken Inasal.

Ingredients

  • 1 chicken piece
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 egg
  • ¼ cup milk or water
  • 2 cups cooking oil (for frying)

Directions

  1. Wash and pat dry the chicken.
  2. Season the chicken with salt, pepper, and garlic powder.
  3. Beat the egg and mix with the milk or water.
  4. Dip the chicken into the egg mixture.
  5. Coat the chicken evenly with flour.
  6. Heat the cooking oil in a pan.
  7. Carefully place the chicken into the hot oil.
  8. Fry until golden brown and fully cooked (about 12–15 minutes, depending on size).
  9. Remove and drain excess oil on paper towels.
