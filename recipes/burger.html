Burger Recipe

Description

A burger is a popular sandwich made with a cooked patty—usually beef, but also chicken, fish, or plant-based—placed inside a sliced bun. It is often topped with ingredients like lettuce, tomato, cheese, onions, pickles, ketchup, mayonnaise, or mustard.

Ingredients

  • 1 burger bun
  • 1 ground beef patty (about 100–150g)
  • 1 slice cheese
  • 1 lettuce leaf
  • 1–2 tomato slices
  • 1–2 onion slices
  • Salt (pinch)
  • Black pepper (pinch)

Directions

  1. Shape the ground beef into 1 patty and season with salt and pepper.
  2. Heat a pan and add a little butter or oil.
  3. Cook the patty for about 3–5 minutes per side (until fully cooked).
  4. Place a slice of cheese on top of the patty and let it melt.
  5. Slice the burger bun in half and lightly toast it on the pan.
  6. Spread ketchup and/or mayonnaise on the bun if desired.
  7. Assemble the burger: bottom bun → lettuce → patty with cheese → tomato → onion → top bun.
  8. Serve immediately while warm.
